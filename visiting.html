Before You Arrive

Plan documents, transportation, lodging, and visitor support before travel.

Travel Documents

Keep passports, Republic States application receipts, residence documents, invitation letters, and lodging information available. Visitors may be asked about travel purpose, destination, and length of stay.

Entry and Customs

Review port-of-entry procedures, declarations, restricted items, and traveler categories before departure. When in doubt, declare goods and ask for guidance.

Airport Arrivals

Felix Lego City International Airport connects visitors to immigration inspection, baggage claim, ground transportation, public transit, and visitor information.

Public Transit

City Metro Transit provides state-owned public transit service for Lego City County, with airport, downtown, civic center, and commuter connections.

Highways and Roads

Major routes connect Death Star City with coastal districts, civic centers, parks, and inland communities. Check alerts before long-distance travel.

Rail Travel

Republic States Railways serves fictional intercity and regional corridors through the Department of Transportation system.

Places to Visit

The Republic States setting includes California-inspired public landscapes, civic buildings, coastlines, and urban districts.

  • Death Star City: seat of national administration, civic offices, public plazas, visitor services, and government records.
  • Lego City County: local government, transit services, airport access, public safety agencies, and community offices.
  • Coastal districts: overlooks, beaches, harbors, scenic routes, visitor centers, and public safety notices.
  • Parks and public lands: forest roads, desert viewpoints, trailheads, picnic areas, and seasonal access notices.
