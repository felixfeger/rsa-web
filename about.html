About the Republic States

The Republic States of America, also called the Republic States, RSA, or RS, is a fictional independent country created for pretend civic and worldbuilding purposes.

This is not a real government organization and has no legal authority. It is a fictional public website for creative use.

Overview

The Republic States of America is presented as an independent republic with national public services, civic information, visitor guidance, and official communications for residents, visitors, and organizations. This website acts as the central public portal for the Republic States Government.

Government

The Republic States Government is responsible for publishing public notices, organizing national service information, maintaining official online guidance, and helping people find the correct office or service. Official online information is provided through approved republicstates.xyz domains.

Capital

The national government is represented at the R.S. Government Building, located at 125 S Death Star City Dr., Death Star City, RS 10003. Death Star City is the fictional seat of national administration for the Republic States.

Public Services

Government services include identity and records support, permits and licensing, public safety information, visitor resources, civic requests, and official website verification. Services are organized to help the public quickly find the right place to start.

Flag and Identity

The Republic States flag uses red and white stripes with a blue canton and stars, based on the uploaded flag image provided for this website. The flag is used as the logo and visual mark for official Republic States pages.

Website Purpose

This website is designed in the style of a government information portal, but it is only for pretend purposes. It should not be used as a real source of legal, emergency, immigration, tax, or public-service authority.
