About the Republic States

The Republic States of America, also called the Republic States, RSA, or RS, is a fictional independent country created for pretend civic, public-information, and worldbuilding purposes.

This is not a real government organization and has no legal authority. It is a fictional public website for creative use.

Overview

A central guide to the fictional Republic States Government.

Public Portal

This website acts as the central public portal for the Republic States Government. It organizes services, visitor information, official website guidance, emergency notices, and contact channels in one place so residents and visitors can find the right starting point.

National Identity

The Republic States is represented as an independent republic with a national capital, government building, official websites, public service offices, and a civic identity built around access to information.

Public Information Principles

The site is written to feel like a public-service portal: plain language, clear categories, and direct links.

  1. Make services easy to find. Pages group information by topics, common tasks, and life events.
  2. Keep official guidance recognizable. The banner, footer, official website checker, and disclaimers help users understand what is official within the fictional setting.
  3. Separate public information from secure forms. Contact messages do not require OTP, while sensitive pretend applications use OTP before submission.
  4. State the pretend status clearly. Legal, immigration, emergency, tax, and safety information on this site is fictional and should not be treated as real public authority.
