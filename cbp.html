Customs and Immigration

Entry guidance, visitor processing, declarations, and border inspection information for travel to the Republic States.

Traveler notice: This Republic States website is for pretend purposes only. It is not a real customs, immigration, passport, visa, or law enforcement authority.

Before You Travel

Visitors should prepare identity documents, travel plans, lodging information, and contact details before arriving at a Republic States port of entry. Travelers may be asked about the purpose and length of their visit.

  • Carry a valid passport or approved Republic States travel document.
  • Keep application receipts, invitation letters, or residency documents available.
  • Review prohibited and restricted items before packing.
  • Use official websites ending in republicstates.xyz or citymetro.xyz for public guidance.

At the Port of Entry

Customs and immigration officers review traveler identity, travel purpose, admissibility, and declared goods. Travelers may be sent to secondary inspection when more information is needed.

  • Follow posted signs for citizens, residents, visitors, and crew.
  • Answer inspection questions clearly and truthfully.
  • Present customs declarations when requested.
  • Keep baggage with you until inspection is complete.

Declarations

Declare food, plants, currency, commercial merchandise, gifts, tools, and items you plan to leave in the Republic States. When unsure, declare the item and ask an officer.

Restricted Items

Weapons, controlled substances, counterfeit goods, unsafe products, protected wildlife items, and certain agricultural goods may be restricted or prohibited.

Visitor Stays

Admission records may include visitor category, entry date, and authorized stay period. Visitors should leave or apply for a change before their authorized stay ends.

Immigration Services

  • Citizenship: start a secure citizenship application with email OTP verification.
  • Permanent residence: apply for a green card for long-term Republic States residence.
  • Travel documents: request a Republic States passport for official pretend travel.
  • Visitor questions: use the contact page for general customs and immigration messages.

Visitors and Tourists

Visitors should be ready to explain where they will stay, how long they plan to remain, and how they will support their visit. Keep hotel reservations, host addresses, return travel plans, and emergency contacts available.

Residents and Returning Citizens

Residents and citizens should present the appropriate Republic States document, answer questions about goods acquired abroad, and update contact or residence records when required.

Business and Commercial Travel

Commercial visitors may need to declare samples, equipment, sale goods, or tools. Businesses should keep invoices, letters of assignment, shipping records, and customs documents organized.

Transit and Airport Arrivals

Travelers connecting through Felix Lego City International Airport should follow airport signage for immigration, customs, baggage claim, public transit, and onward travel.
